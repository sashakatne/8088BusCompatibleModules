Known waveform artifacts (not bugs):
  - Narrow glitch pulses on M0_CS / M1_CS between bus operations, and a
    brief M0_CS blip near 2900 ns during an I/O cycle (visible in
    waveform_config1_moore_iptb.png), are combinational decoder hazards
    on the chip-select equations in tb/top_interface.sv:42-47.
    bus.IOM and bus.Address are both inputs to the pure-combinational
    decode, and both change in successive delta cycles while ALE is
    high and the 8282 latch is transparent.
  - These are filtered functionally because ControlSequencer
    (rtl/memorio.sv:90-98) samples CS only at the posedge CLK when in
    INIT, so intra-cycle glitches are absorbed by the flip-flop.
  - Empirical confirmation: configurations 2 and 4 (self-checking TB
    against the same decoder) end in '*** PASSED ***' with zero
    'Error: Read data ... does not match ...' lines.
  - See README.md, section "Chip-Select Glitches: A Combinational
    Decode Hazard", for the full analysis.
